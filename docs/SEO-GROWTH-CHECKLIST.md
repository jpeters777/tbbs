# TBBS SEO & growth checklists

**Goal:** Become the top-searched **local** cosmetic surgery site for Tampa Bay money queries (Lipo 360, BBL, body contouring, tummy tuck, gynecomastia, brand) — and turn that traffic into `/contact` leads.

**North star metrics (check monthly):**
- Google Search Console **28-day** clicks on money queries ↑
- `/contact` form submissions ↑
- Ignore: one-day CTR swings, impression-only vanity

**Rules:** Stay on `www`. Do not rename URLs casually. Lead CTAs go to `/contact`, not `/consult`. Cherry-only financing on site. No doctor names / old B&A photos without approval.

---

## Daily (weekdays) — ~15 minutes

Do these in order:

1. **Leads** — Reply to every new inquiry same day. Book calls for anyone warm.
2. **Old-lead bump (only if due)** — If a Monday bump list is ready, send the short “still interested?” text. Do not re-blast the long pitch.
3. **Instagram** — At least one Story or feed action that points to `https://www.tampabaybodysculpting.com/contact` (Fall special through **Sept 30, 2026**, or a money procedure).
4. **Site sanity** — Spot-check homepage or one money page loads; phone `813-557-6400` still correct.
5. **Stop** — Do not open GSC every day. Do not rewrite titles daily.

---

## Weekly (Mondays) — ~45 minutes

1. **GSC — 28 days, Queries** — Note top brand + money queries. Flag any with high impressions + low clicks.
2. **GSC — 28 days, Pages** — Which URLs get impressions? Which get clicks?
3. **One SEO task only** — Pick the single highest-ROI fix (title/meta, FAQ, internal link, or content gap). Do not start five projects.
4. **Social plan for the week** — 2–3 posts/Stories: Fall special + 1–2 procedures (Lipo 360, BBL, Lipo 360 + BBL, gynecomastia, etc.). All → `/contact`.
5. **Lead list hygiene** — Remove opt-outs. Note who needs a call vs text.
6. **Ship or park** — If a site change is done, ask to commit/push. Otherwise leave it for next week.

---

## Monthly (first Monday) — ~90 minutes

1. **Scorecard** — Compare this month vs last: GSC clicks, contact leads, top 10 queries.
2. **Money-page pass** — Homepage, `/liposuction`, `/liposuction-360`, `/lipo-360-bbl`, `/brazilian-butt-lift-bbl`, `/tummy-tuck`, `/male-chest-reduction`, `/mommy-makeover`. Titles still match demand?
3. **Indexing / redirects** — No important 404s on money URLs; www canonical still correct.
4. **Speed** — Quick mobile check on homepage + one procedure page (feels fast, hero loads).
5. **Decide one new page or none** — Only if a query/patients prove demand. Prefer strengthening existing pages.
6. **Reviews** — Ask happy patients for Google reviews (brand + maps trust).

## Never do (SEO traps)

- Keyword-stuff every page
- Rebuild the whole site for rankings
- Chase national “#1 in the industry” vanity
- Send ads/Stories to `/consult`
- Rename URLs without a redirect plan
- Expect title tweaks to move rankings in days (give **2–4 weeks**)

## When an agent reminds you

The reminder should:
1. Tell you **today’s date** and which checklist applies (daily / weekly / monthly).
2. Give a **short ordered checklist** for today only.
3. Call out **seasonal items** (Fall special) if still active.
4. Not invent new site projects unless GSC data in the message supports them.
5. Ask if you want help executing any item in Cursor.
